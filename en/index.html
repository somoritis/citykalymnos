Our apartments are fully renovated, located in the center of the island, in a quiet traditional neighborhood of Kalymnos, in Pothia. Within a 30-100 meter radius you'll find restaurants, cafes, a supermarket, bakery, shops, pharmacies, museums, and a taxi and bus stop. A 3-5 minute walk brings you to Vouvali beach, and 5-10 minutes in the other direction to Kasonia beach. Ideal apartments and rooms for accommodation at the port of Kalymnos — fully equipped, with capacity for up to 5 guests.
